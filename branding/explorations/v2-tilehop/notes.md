# v2-tilehop

Two rounded window tiles sit side by side. A short, thick, round-capped arc hops from the lilac source to the violet destination. Three grey diagonal ticks mark the departure. This is the Alt+Tab move without a keycap, text, or arrow.

Palette: graphite #16181d; white-lilac #e8e6ff; electric violet #8b7bff; mid-grey #6b7080. On light backgrounds: ink #16181d, deep violet #6a5af0, grey #5b5f6e. The stencil uses black #000000 on transparency.

Shared with omagnet: flat family colours, smooth rounded geometry, a single violet element that has moved, generous clear space, and the graphite tile with a 97 px radius on a 512 px canvas (18.95%). The three motion ticks copy the finished glyph's 18 by 18 diagonal displacement, 9 px stroke, 19 px vertical spacing, and round caps, translated to the start of the hop. The 48 px arc has the thick, calm weight of the ring arms. No gradients, shadows, filters, text, or embedded bitmaps occur in the SVGs.

Different idea: two solid window tiles connected visually by an airborne arc, rather than four quarters forming an open ring. Colour is supplementary: the separate tiles and arc survive in the one-colour stencil.

16 px inspection: the two tiles remain distinct and the arch survives. The three ticks become a faint grey texture rather than three reliably countable strokes. The arch can also suggest a handle or headphones at this size, so the hop reading is clearer at larger sizes. Family resemblance is strong in the palette, rounded silhouette, and motion detail.

The SVG files are hand-written vector masters. icon-512.png and icon-16.png were exported directly from icon.svg with rsvg-convert. All four SVGs were parsed and rendered successfully.

concept.png is the built-in image generation concept resized to 1024 by 1024. codex-first.png preserves the first raw output byte for byte. The generated concept is a visual exploration; the SVG is the precise palette and geometry master.

Image prompt: logo-brand, 1024 square flat app icon, graphite rounded tile, two solid rounded square tiles (left lilac, right violet), thick lilac round-capped hop arch above, three short parallel diagonal grey round-capped departure ticks. Match omagnet construction and family palette. Calm abstract Alt+Tab movement. No text, arrows, rings, gradients, shadows, glows, texture, or extra shapes.
